2. What data we handle

When you browse the site

The site does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, social media plug-ins, embedded videos or maps. Fonts are hosted on our own site, so your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other third-party service when you visit.

A few small items are stored in your own browser:

  • your cart (the designs and options you chose), so it is still there when you change page or come back later;
  • your light/dark theme choice, if you use the theme switch;
  • if you have an account, a random session code that keeps you signed in (it is sent to our server when you use your account or place an order) and, while you sign in, the kit you were adding to the cart.

See the cookie policy for details and how to clear them.

Like every web server, the companies that host this site and our API (GitHub, Inc. – GitHub Pages, and Cloudflare, Inc. – Cloudflare Pages, Workers and D1) receive the technical data needed to deliver pages and answer requests, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in short-lived technical logs for security.

When you place an order

At checkout we ask only for what we need to deliver your order: full name, email address, shipping address and country, the order contents and, if you enter it, your NIF (Portuguese tax number, optional, for the invoice). Card details are entered directly with our payment provider, Stripe, and are never seen or stored by us. While you are on the payment page the order is kept as a draft; it only becomes an order once the payment is confirmed.

When you create an account

An account is needed to buy. It works without a password: we email you a sign-in link. We store your email address, the dates you created the account and last signed in, your language and, if you fill them in on your profile, your name, phone and address. When you create the account we ask for your date of birth to confirm you are at least 13; the account is only created if you are. We also log sign-in link requests (email and time) to prevent abuse.

When you contact us

If you email us (for example about a custom design, a question, a return or a complaint), we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach.

3. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • To process and deliver your order, and handle returns and guarantee claims: necessary to perform our contract with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • To issue invoices and keep accounting records: necessary to comply with Portuguese tax and accounting law (art. 6(1)(c)).
  • To answer your messages: steps you asked for before a contract (art. 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in answering you (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep the website secure and working (hosting logs): our legitimate interest in running a secure website (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To check the minimum age for creating an account: our legitimate interest in not creating accounts for children under 13 (art. 6(1)(f)). No account is created for anyone under 13. The account itself is necessary to perform the contract (art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Keeping your cart and theme in your browser: needed for the shopping function you asked for; it stays on your device.

We do not send marketing emails. If we ever offer a newsletter, it will be opt-in and you will be able to withdraw your consent at any time.
