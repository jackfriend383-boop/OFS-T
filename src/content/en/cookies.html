Cookie policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. In short

This website does not set any cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising, no social media plug-ins, no third-party embeds and no externally hosted fonts. It stores a few small items in your browser's storage so the shop works the way you expect. They stay on your device; only the account session code is sent to our server, when you use your account.

None of these items contains your name or email address. Stripe's payment page (on stripe.com) sets its own cookies under Stripe's policy.

3. Why there is no cookie banner

EU and Portuguese law (the ePrivacy Directive, art. 5(3), transposed by Law 41/2004) require consent before storing information on your device, except where it is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly asked for. Keeping your cart and remembering a display setting you chose fall under that exemption, and we use nothing else, so we do not show a consent banner. If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential technology, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

4. How to clear or block it

You can remove these items at any time:

  • Emptying your cart removes the kits stored in amig-cart.
  • Clearing “cookies and site data” for this website in your browser settings removes all of these items. In most browsers this is under Settings → Privacy (or Privacy and security) → Cookies and site data.
  • Private or incognito windows delete them automatically when you close the window.

If you block local storage, the site still works, but your cart will not be remembered between pages and the theme will follow your device setting.
