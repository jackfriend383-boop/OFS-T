Our story

OFS/T started with one black Ami and a roll of leftover vinyl. The car is small, square and mostly flat, which makes it the best canvas on the road. We design every kit around its exact panels, so the cut lines follow the door badge, the panel seams and the window edge.

Kits are printed and plotter-cut in small batches. The full kit covers both door panels and both rear quarter windows, and single pieces are sold in pairs.

Frequently asked questions

How long does fitting take?

It depends on your experience, so take your time. Clean the panel, position the vinyl before removing the backing, and squeegee from the centre outwards, following the step-by-step fitting guide.

Can I take it through a car wash?

Follow the care instructions in the fitting guide. As a general rule for vehicle vinyl, give it a few days to settle after fitting before washing, and keep pressure-washer nozzles away from the edges.

Can I remove it later?

Yes, vehicle vinyl is designed to be removable. Warm it gently with a hairdryer and peel slowly at a low angle. How easily it comes off depends on its age, sun exposure and the surface, so try a corner first.

Does it fit other cars?

Kits are cut for the Citroën Ami. If you want to fit one to another model, or your Ami has aftermarket trim, please contact us before ordering.

Can I get my own design?

Send us a sketch or a vector file and we'll quote a custom kit, including the production time. Custom kits are made just for you, so they cannot be returned for a change of mind (faulty kits are always covered).
