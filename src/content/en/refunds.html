Sales & returns policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This page explains how we take payment and deliver, when you can cancel an order, how refunds work, and what happens if a kit is faulty. It is part of our terms of sale and does not affect your statutory rights.

1. How to order and pay

Payment is 100% due when you place the order, and we only produce once payment is confirmed.

  • Payment methods: those offered on Stripe's payment page (for example card and MB WAY).
  • Price: the total price, including delivery costs, is shown before you pay.
  • Confirmation: the final button states that the order means a payment, and we send a confirmation by email.

2. Timing and delivery

Delivery is in person, with no delivery charge, on the island of São Miguel (Azores), within 14 to 21 days of payment being confirmed. For drop pre-orders, the time counts from the close of the order window.

  • Updates: if the timing changes, we tell you as soon as we know.
  • Delays: if we cannot deliver within the stated time, you can cancel the order and get a full refund.
  • Outside São Miguel: for now we do not deliver outside this area. The site says so before you buy, and if you live elsewhere you can join the waiting list.

3. 14-day right of withdrawal

For catalogue designs and colour choices, you can withdraw from the purchase within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason, under Decree-Law 24/2014.

  • How: send a clear statement to geral@ofstdesigns.com, or use the model form below (optional).
  • Condition of the product: the sticker must be returned unused and in its packaging. A sticker that has already been applied cannot be returned in that state. Do not peel the backing or apply any part of the kit if you want to withdraw: you can inspect the kit as you would in a shop, but you may be liable for any loss in value of a kit handled more than necessary.

4. Custom orders

You can ask for a made-to-measure design, with text, a name or an adaptation to your car, at an extra cost. These requests are confirmed in writing before production.

  • No withdrawal: because the sticker is made to your specifications, there is no right of withdrawal (Decree-Law 24/2014, art. 17(1)(c)). You are told this before you pay and confirm that you accept it.
  • Before production: we may cancel with a full refund a custom order that has not yet been sent to print.
  • Defects: this exclusion does not affect your rights if the product is faulty.

5. Refunds

We refund by the same payment method used for the purchase, at no charge to you.

  • Timing: within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal statement. We may wait for the product to come back, or for proof that you returned it, before refunding.
  • Return costs: paid by the customer, who is told this here, before buying. As we deliver in person, we arrange the return of the kit with you.
  • Cancellation before production: full refund.
  • No refund: on custom orders already produced, unless faulty.

6. Inspection and defects

Every kit is inspected before it is handed over. We do not offer an additional commercial guarantee and, once the customer has fitted the sticker, we are not responsible for damage caused by misuse or mistreatment (for example a pressure washer aimed at the edges).

  • Defect on delivery: if the kit arrives faulty or not as ordered, email photos to geral@ofstdesigns.com with your order number, preferably before fitting it. We replace the kit or, where the law requires, refund you.
  • Legal rights: this does not affect your consumer rights, including the legal guarantee of conformity set by law.
  • Details: see the terms of sale, sections 9 and 10.

7. Problems on delivery

If the kit has a problem at the time of delivery:

  1. Tell us straight away, or photograph the kit and packaging before throwing anything away.
  2. Email geral@ofstdesigns.com with your order number and the photos, preferably within [NUMBER] days, so we can look into the problem.

We replace the kit or refund you. Reporting later does not take away your rights under the legal guarantee.
