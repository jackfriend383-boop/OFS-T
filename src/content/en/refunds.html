Refunds & returns

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This page explains when you can cancel an order and return a kit, how refunds work, and what happens if a kit is faulty or arrives damaged. It is part of our terms of sale and does not affect your statutory rights.

1. 14-day right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer and bought a standard kit, you can withdraw from the contract within 14 days without giving a reason, under Decree-Law 24/2014. The 14 days start on the day you, or someone you name other than the carrier, receive the kit. If an order arrives in several parcels, they start when the last parcel arrives.

2. Personalised kits cannot be returned for a change of mind

The right of withdrawal does not apply to goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalised (Decree-Law 24/2014, art. 17(1)(c)). Every kit is cut to order, but we treat it as follows:

  • Standard kit (14-day withdrawal applies): any design in its default colours (the colours shown when you open the design, or the original colours of fixed-colour artwork), in matte or gloss, for one or both sides, without badge text.
  • Personalised kit (no withdrawal for a change of mind): a kit with custom badge text, a kit in a colour combination you chose that differs from the design's default colours, or a custom design made from your own sketch or artwork.

We show whether a kit is personalised before you order. Personalised kits are still fully covered by the legal guarantee if they are faulty or not as ordered (see section 6).

4. Sending the kit back

Send the kit back to [RETURN ADDRESS] within 14 days of telling us you are withdrawing. [CONFIRM: You pay the direct cost of returning the kit / We pay the return postage.]

You may look at the kit as you would in a shop. Please do not peel the backing or apply any part of it: once vinyl has been applied it usually cannot be reused, and you may be liable for the reduced value of a kit that was handled more than needed to check it.

5. Refunds

We refund all payments received from you, including the standard delivery cost, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal notice. We may hold the refund until we have received the kit back or you have shown proof that you sent it, whichever is earlier. If you chose a more expensive delivery option than our standard one, we refund only the cost of standard delivery.

We refund using the same payment method you used, unless you expressly agree otherwise; you will not pay any fee for the refund.

6. Faulty or wrong items

If a kit is faulty, incomplete or not what you ordered, it is covered by the legal guarantee of conformity for three years from delivery. This applies to standard and personalised kits alike. Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with your order number and photos. We will repair or replace it at no cost to you, including postage, or, where the law allows, reduce the price or refund you.

7. Damaged in transit

If the parcel or kit arrives damaged:

  1. If you can, note the damage with the carrier when you accept the parcel.
  2. Take photos of the packaging, the label and the damaged parts before throwing anything away.
  3. Email us at [CONTACT EMAIL] with your order number and the photos, ideally within [NUMBER] days, so we can claim from the carrier.

We will send a replacement or refund you. Reporting later does not remove your rights under the legal guarantee; it only makes the carrier claim harder.
