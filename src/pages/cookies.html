Cookie policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

1. In short

This website does not set any cookies. It uses no analytics, no advertising, no social media plug-ins, no third-party embeds and no externally hosted fonts. It stores two small items in your browser's local storage so the shop works the way you expect; they stay on your device and are never sent to us.

2. What we store on your device

NameTypePurposeHow long
amig-cartLocal storage, strictly necessaryRemembers the kits and options in your cart while you move between pages and when you come back.Until you empty the cart or clear your browser data
ofst-themeLocal storage, functional preferenceRemembers whether you chose the light or dark theme. Only set if you use the theme switch.Until you clear your browser data

Neither item contains your name, email address or any identifier, and the site has no code that reads them back to a server.

3. Why there is no cookie banner

EU and Portuguese law (the ePrivacy Directive, art. 5(3), transposed by Law 41/2004) require consent before storing information on your device, except where it is strictly necessary to provide a service you explicitly asked for. Keeping your cart and remembering a display setting you chose fall under that exemption, and we use nothing else, so we do not show a consent banner. If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential technology, we will ask for your consent first and update this page.

4. How to clear or block it

You can remove these items at any time:

  • Emptying your cart removes the kits stored in amig-cart.
  • Clearing “cookies and site data” for this website in your browser settings removes both items. In most browsers this is under Settings → Privacy (or Privacy and security) → Cookies and site data.
  • Private or incognito windows delete them automatically when you close the window.

If you block local storage, the site still works, but your cart will not be remembered between pages and the theme will follow your device setting.
