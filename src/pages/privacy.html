  • To process and deliver your order, and handle returns and guarantee claims: necessary to perform our contract with you (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).

  • To answer your messages: steps you asked for before a contract (art. 6(1)(b)), or our legitimate interest in answering you (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To keep the website secure and working (hosting logs): our legitimate interest in running a secure website (art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Keeping your cart and theme in your browser: needed for the shopping function you asked for; it stays on your device.

We do not send marketing emails. If we ever offer a newsletter, it will be opt-in and you will be able to withdraw your consent at any time.
